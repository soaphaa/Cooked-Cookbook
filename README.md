## About

Cooked Cookbook is a recipe website made to share our group members' personal recipes in an accessable way to everyone. There are additional features that are all designed with a storng focus on UI/UX.
Unlike most recipe websites cluttered with ads hard to follow layouts, Cooked Cookbook is built to actually improve your cooking experience !!

---

## Features

- Search and filter recipes by ingredient or tag
- Built-in cooking timer and nutrition list
- Ingredient checklist panel with recipe scaling options
- Your own profile to track your recipe completion aswell as saved recipes

- Star reviews for each recipe
